## Design Context

### Users
Music listeners who prefer local libraries over streaming. They care about having a calm, distraction-free desktop player that doesn't fight for attention. They value quiet tools that stay out of the way. Context: late-night listening sessions, personal music collections, a desire for software that matches the mood of their music.

### Brand Personality
Calm, intimate, unhurried. Shiranami is a sanctuary — software that feels like a quiet room with good headphones. Not trying to impress, just trying to be comfortable.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Vibe**: Lofi, late-night, cozy. Think lo-fi beats girl aesthetic — soft glows, muted tones, quiet confidence. The page should feel like 2am with headphones on.
- **Theme**: Dark mode only. Dark lavender/purple palette (oklch hue ~280). No harsh contrasts.
- **Typography**: Sora for display (geometric, soft), DM Sans for body (warm, readable).
- **Anti-references**: Corporate SaaS templates, AI-generated hero sections with gradient text and glowing cards, anything that screams "startup landing page". No fake urgency, no flashy animations.
- **Reference**: ShiroAni landing page (same dev) — simple, comfy, intentional. The Shiranami page should be even quieter.

### Design Principles
1. **Quiet over loud** — Every element should earn its space. If it fights for attention, remove it.
2. **Warmth through restraint** — Coziness comes from soft spacing, muted colors, and breathing room — not from adding more stuff.
3. **Honest craft** — No placeholder content, no dev notes on production pages, no "coming soon" filler. Ship what's real.
4. **Lofi texture** — Subtle imperfections and organic touches (the divider animation, grain overlay) give life without being distracting.
5. **One mood** — Everything should feel like the same room. The nav, hero, features, footer — all part of one continuous, calm space.
